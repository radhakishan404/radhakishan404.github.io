What Is ViMax?

Director, Screenwriter, Producer & Video Generator — All-in-One Director, Screenwriter, Producer & Video Generator — Sab Ek Mein

Most AI video tools generate 5-second clips with characters that change faces mid-video. ViMax is different. It's an agentic system — meaning multiple AI agents work together like a film production crew. You give it an idea. It writes the script, designs storyboards, plans camera angles, creates consistent characters, and generates the final video. End to end. Automatically.

Zyaadatar AI video tools bas 5-second ki clips banate hain aur characters ke face bich mein change ho jaate hain. ViMax alag hai. Ye ek agentic system hai — matlab multiple AI agents milke kaam karte hain jaise ek film production crew. Tum ek idea dete ho. Ye script likhta hai, storyboard design karta hai, camera angles plan karta hai, consistent characters banata hai, aur final video generate karta hai. End to end. Automatically.

Key Features

4 Ways to Create Videos Video Banane Ke 4 Tarike

ViMax gives you multiple entry points — from raw ideas to full screenplays, even novels and your own photos.

ViMax tumhe multiple entry points deta hai — raw ideas se lekar full screenplays, novels aur tumhari apni photos tak.

💡

Idea2Video

Type a simple idea like "a cat and dog are best friends, what happens when they meet a stranger" — and ViMax generates the complete video. Script, shots, characters, everything.

Ek simple idea likho jaise "ek cat aur dog best friends hain, kya hoga jab wo ek stranger se milte hain" — aur ViMax complete video generate karta hai. Script, shots, characters, sab kuch.

📝

Script2Video

Already have a screenplay? Write your scenes with dialogue, character descriptions, and settings. ViMax handles all the visual production — storyboard, images, video.

Screenplay already ready hai? Apne scenes likho dialogue, character descriptions, aur settings ke saath. ViMax saari visual production handle karta hai.

📚

Novel2Video

Feed it an entire novel. ViMax intelligently compresses the narrative, tracks characters across chapters, and generates episodic video content scene by scene.

Ek poori novel feed karo. ViMax intelligently narrative ko compress karta hai, characters ko chapters mein track karta hai, aur episodic video content generate karta hai.

🤳

AutoCameo

This is wild. Upload your own photo and ViMax puts you inside the video as a character — fully consistent from start to finish. Your face, your appearance, maintained across every single scene.

Ye feature insane hai. Apni photo upload karo aur ViMax tumhe video mein character bana deta hai — start se finish tak fully consistent. Tumhara face, tumhara look, har scene mein same.

How It Works

Multi-Agent Production Pipeline Multi-Agent Production Pipeline

ViMax uses specialized AI agents working together — like a real film crew. Here's the pipeline from your idea to the final video:

ViMax specialized AI agents use karta hai jo saath mein kaam karte hain — jaise ek real film crew. Yahan hai pipeline tumhare idea se final video tak:

1

Script Understanding Agent

Script Understanding Agent

Extracts characters, environments, emotions, and scene boundaries from your input. Understands what the story needs.

Tumhare input se characters, environments, emotions, aur scene boundaries extract karta hai. Samajhta hai ki story ko kya chahiye.

2

Storyboard Designer

Storyboard Designer

Creates shot-level storyboards with cinematographic language — wide shots, close-ups, transitions, pacing, camera movements.

Shot-level storyboards banata hai cinematographic language ke saath — wide shots, close-ups, transitions, pacing, camera movements.

3

Visual Asset Planner

Visual Asset Planner

Intelligently selects and manages reference images. Tracks what characters and environments looked like in previous scenes.

Intelligently reference images select aur manage karta hai. Track karta hai ki characters aur environments pehle ke scenes mein kaise dikhte the.

4

Consistency Engine

Consistency Engine

Generates multiple image candidates in parallel. Uses VLM (Vision Language Model) to pick the most consistent one. Like a human director saying "take 3 was the best."

Multiple image candidates parallel mein generate karta hai. VLM (Vision Language Model) use karke most consistent wala choose karta hai. Jaise ek human director bolta hai "take 3 sabse accha tha."

5

Video Synthesis & Assembly

Video Synthesis & Assembly

Parallel shot generation for maximum speed. Final assembly with timeline, cuts, and transitions. Output: your complete video.

Maximum speed ke liye parallel shot generation. Final assembly timeline, cuts, aur transitions ke saath. Output: tumhari complete video.

Quick Start

Get Running in 5 Minutes 5 Minute Mein Shuru Karo

Requirements: Linux or Windows, Python 3.10+, and the uv package manager.

Requirements: Linux ya Windows, Python 3.10+, aur uv package manager.

Step 1 — Clone & Install Step 1 — Clone & Install

terminal git clone https://github.com/HKUDS/ViMax.git
cd ViMax
uv sync

Step 2 — Configure API Keys Step 2 — API Keys Configure Karo

Edit configs/idea2video.yaml:

configs/idea2video.yaml edit karo:

yaml chat_model:
  init_args:
    model: google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite
    model_provider: openai
    api_key: YOUR_API_KEY
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1

image_generator:
  class_path: tools.ImageGeneratorNanobananaGoogleAPI
  init_args:
    api_key: YOUR_GOOGLE_API_KEY

video_generator:
  class_path: tools.VideoGeneratorVeoGoogleAPI
  init_args:
    api_key: YOUR_GOOGLE_API_KEY

Step 3 — Generate Your First Video Step 3 — Pehli Video Generate Karo

Edit main_idea2video.py with your idea:

main_idea2video.py mein apna idea likho:

python idea = """
A lonely astronaut discovers music on a distant planet
"""

user_requirement = """
Cinematic feel, 4-5 scenes, dramatic lighting
"""

style = "Cinematic Realism"
terminal uv run python main_idea2video.py

That's it. ViMax will generate the script, design storyboards, create reference images, check consistency, render shots, and assemble the final video — all automatically.

Bas itna hi. ViMax script generate karega, storyboards design karega, reference images banayega, consistency check karega, shots render karega, aur final video assemble karega — sab automatic.
