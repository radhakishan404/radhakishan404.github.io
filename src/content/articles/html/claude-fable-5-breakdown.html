What is Fable 5 — and what is Mythos?

For over a year, Anthropic was running a secret model internally called Mythos. It was only available to a handful of trusted cybersecurity researchers and organisations through a programme called Project Glasswing. The public could not touch it.

On June 9, 2026, Anthropic released two things at once:

For the public
Claude Fable 5 — the same underlying Mythos model, but with additional safety guardrails added on top. This is what you can use at claude.ai right now.
For trusted organisations only
Claude Mythos 5 — the unrestricted version. Shared only with pre-approved partners. Not available to the public.

Fable 5 is not just a better version of Opus or Sonnet. It sits in a completely different tier — what Anthropic calls "Mythos-class." Think of it as a new category above everything they have released before.

"This is a super exciting release. The benchmarks are great and it's SOTA on everything by a margin — but I'll add that qualitatively also, this is a major-version-bump-deserving step change forward." — Andrej Karpathy, AI researcher (ex-Tesla, ex-OpenAI)

The Pokémon FireRed test

To demonstrate Fable 5's vision capabilities, Anthropic let it play Pokémon FireRed — using only raw game screenshots. No maps. No navigation aids. No helper tools that earlier models needed.

Previous models needed a special "harness" of helper tools just to navigate the game. Fable 5 looked at the screen like a human would and figured it out from sight alone.

This is not just a fun party trick. It is showing that Fable 5 can look at any complex visual interface — a dashboard, a design file, a data chart — and understand what is happening without needing it explained in text.

Also noteworthy Fable 5 played Slay the Spire 3x better than Opus 4.8 using its file-based memory system — saving its own notes between moves to build strategy across a long game session.

The Stripe codebase story

Stripe — one of the world's largest payments companies — gave Fable 5 a codebase with 50 million lines of code and asked it to handle a migration task.

The task would have taken a team of engineers roughly two months. Fable 5 completed it in one day.

This is the clearest signal of what "Mythos-class" actually means in practice. It is not about being slightly smarter at answering questions. It is about being able to hold an entire large project in its head, understand all the dependencies, and execute complex changes without breaking things.
